The Voice Isn't a Fact

Self-doubt sounds like the truth for one simple reason: it is the voice you have listened to the longest. It says "you're not ready", "they'll think it's stupid", "everyone else has it figured out", and after years of hearing it, it feels like a fact.

It isn't. It is a habit of thought, and a habit can be tested.

Your goal today is not to silence the voice. It is to stop letting it make your decisions. Doubt can ride along. It just doesn't get the wheel.
1

Catch it 2 MIN

Write down three doubts you told yourself this week, word for word, exactly the way the voice says them.

2

Test it 2 MIN

Next to each one write F if it is a Fact you can prove, R if it is a Fear about what might happen, or H if it is an old Habit of thought. Most doubts turn out to be fears and habits.

3

Rewrite it 1 MIN

Turn each one into: "I notice I'm thinking ______. What is one small step I can take anyway?"

Your first rep

One small step today

Self-trust is built the way a muscle is built: with small reps, not big motivation. Pick one and do it in the next five minutes.

  • ✓Send the message you keep rewriting, in its rough version.
  • ✓Say one opinion out loud that you would normally swallow.
  • ✓Make one small decision without asking anybody.

It does not have to go well. It only has to happen. That is the rep.
