# Regional resources: USA, Canada, UK (dating after 35)

Research date: 2026-10-13. Method: WebSearch only (standard mode). WebFetch was not used, so official pages were NOT opened; everything below comes from search-result snippets/summaries, often via secondary sources (directories, news, councils). Treat "verified in search result" as "seen in a search result", not "confirmed on the official site today". Re-check every number and hours before publishing. Nothing here is from memory unless marked.

Status labels: **V** = verified in search result (source named). **NV** = not verified - check before publishing.

## Recent changes to flag

- UK: Report Fraud replaced Action Fraud on 4 Dec 2025 for England, Wales and Northern Ireland; phone number 0300 123 2040 reported unchanged; Scotland still uses Police Scotland on 101 (V: gov.uk news story and reportfraud.police.uk "goes live" notice, per search summary; Which? also covered it). Do not publish "Action Fraud" as current.
- US: the 988 "Press 3" LGBTQ+ youth option ended 17 July 2025 (V: SAMHSA statement as reported by CentraCare, Health Policy Ohio, news outlets). 988 itself continues. Veterans (Press 1) and Spanish (Press 2) options reported unaffected. Anything after July 2025 not checked.
- Canada: 9-8-8 launched 30 Nov 2023 (V: news/directory listings). Run by CAMH, funded by PHAC. Not checked for 2026 changes.
- UK 159: bank list changes over time; older alerts list fewer banks (V).
- Men's Advice Line hours conflict between sources; Cruse and CAFC hours also conflict.

## 1. Reporting romance scams / online fraud

### USA
- FTC ReportFraud: reportfraud.ftc.gov - V (named in WBAY/12News-type consumer articles; FTC page not itself in results). Phone not found - NV.
- FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3): www.ic3.gov - V (FBI field-office press release in results). FBI advice: stop contact, tell your financial institution immediately about any transfer, file with IC3.
- Stats seen (cite with year, secondary or FBI office): IC3 2022: 19,000+ romance complaints, almost $740M losses (FBI New York page, V). FTC: nearly 70,000 reports and $1.3B lost in 2022 (secondary article, V); $1.14B in 2023 (local news citing FTC, V). Agencies count differently. 2024/2025 figures not found.

### Canada
- Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (CAFC): toll-free 1-888-495-8501; reporting page antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/report-signalez-eng.htm. V via directory listings (211 Saskatchewan, Healthline, CIOC), not the CAFC site. Hours conflicting (most say Mon-Fri 10:00-16:45 ET; one says 9:00 start) - NV on hours.
- RCMP advice: report any scam to the CAFC even if not a victim; contact local police if money lost (V: rcmp-grc.gc.ca).
- Stats: CAFC counted over $58.4M lost to romance fraud in 2024 (Shelburne Free Press, secondary, V); $50M in 2023 (CTV News headline, V).

### UK
- Report Fraud: reportfraud.police.uk, 0300 123 2040 (England, Wales, NI). V. Scotland: 101. V. Report Fraud does not investigate cases or give progress updates (V, one source).
- Bank fraud line 159 (Stop Scams UK): dial 159 to reach your own bank's fraud team; you start the call, 159 never calls you; cost per your provider; stopscamsuk.org.uk/159. V. Check the bank list on the official page.
- Which?: published explainer "Report Fraud: what does this new era of fraud reporting mean" (V, title/URL only). No Which? romance-scam phone line found.
- Emergency 999 for immediate danger (V, councils).

## 3. Domestic abuse / safety

### USA
- National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-SAFE (7233); text START to 88788; video phone for Deaf callers 855-812-1001; thehotline.org; 24/7; 200+ languages per a directory. V (thehotline.org pages in results). Site warns internet use can be monitored. Men-specific US line: not searched - NV.

### Canada
- No single national hotline (V: Women and Gender Equality Canada "Get help now" page links to provincial/territorial lines). Emergency 911.
- ShelterSafe.ca: online map of shelters, each with a 24-hour number; sheltersafe.ca/get-help. V. Shelter counts differ (600+ vs 350+ member shelters) - do not quote a count.
- Examples seen on the Canada.ca page: Alberta Family Violence Info Line 310-1818; Victim Link BC 1-800-563-0808. V.
- Assaulted Women's Helpline (Ontario only, women 16+): 1-866-863-0511, TTY 1-866-863-7868, #7233 on Bell/Rogers/Fido/Telus mobile; 24/7. V (directory listings, not the AWHL site). Do not present as national.
- Men-specific Canadian service: none found - no source found.
- Other-province lines: not searched - NV.

### UK
- National Domestic Abuse Helpline (run by Refuge): 0808 2000 247, free, 24/7. V (council/PCC/Bristol Uni pages).
- Men's Advice Line: 0808 801 0327, for men experiencing abuse from a partner or ex. V, but hours conflict (Mon-Fri 9am-8pm vs older 10-1/2-5) - NV on hours.
- If you cannot speak on 999: press 55 (council page, V - single source).

## 4. Relationship and grief support

- Relate (UK): relate.org.uk, local centres set fees. Seen: roughly GBP 48-88 per session (Hereford/Worcester, Severn and Wye); GBP 60-65 (Coventry/Warwickshire); GBP 92 standard after assessment at one London-area centre; sliding scale and bursaries at some centres. V for local pages; prices dated, NV for current fees.
- Cruse Bereavement Support (UK): helpline 0808 808 1677; free; volunteer-based; hours conflict (9:30-3 Mon-Fri vs longer, Tuesday evening) - number V (listings), hours NV. cruse.org.uk.
- Widowed and Young (WAY, UK): peer support for people widowed at 50 or under (or before 51 - sources differ); membership via signup. V; check eligibility wording. Not suited to all readers over 35.
- DivorceCare (US/Canada): church-hosted 13-week video groups; find at divorcecare.org; fee varies (one listing $20, some free). V (church/community listings).
- GriefShare (US/Canada): same format for death bereavement; griefshare.org; US phone 800-395-5755 appeared in a listing. V (secondary), phone NV. Faith-based - note for secular readers.
- Bereaved Families of Ontario - Toronto: free peer-led bereavement support, 416-440-0290. V (Healthline listing), local only.
- US widowed-specific free service: no source found. Canada national widowed or divorce counselling directory: no source found. Relate has no verified phone number in results.

## 5. Official online-dating safety guidance: red flags named

Caveat: FTC, CAFC, Report Fraud and NCSC pages did not appear in results. Lists below come from secondary reports of those agencies or from other official bodies, so label accordingly.

- US (FTC, via local news; FBI via field-office releases):
  - Professes love or marriage fast, before meeting.
  - Always has excuses not to meet or video call.
  - Asks for money, especially crypto, wire or gift cards.
  - Claims to be military/overseas/oil rig; invents emergencies (sick relative, jail).
  - Pitches "investment" teaching.
  - Stolen or AI photos (reverse image search), thin online presence, typos.
  - FBI: wants to move off the dating site to email/messaging immediately.
- Canada (RCMP page, police services; Canadian Centre for Cyber Security getcybersafe.gc.ca "Spot romantic red flags" PDFs in results):
  - Declares love before meeting; love bombing within days or weeks.
  - Pushes to move to private channels.
  - Always has a reason not to meet or video call; blurry, recycled photos.
  - Says they live nearby but are overseas.
  - Urgent money requests (travel, medical, family); crypto investment pitches.
  - Discourages you from telling friends or family.
- UK: Report Fraud and NCSC guidance NOT found in results - NV, check before publishing. Essex Police (via essex.gov.uk) names overseas military/medical claims; Europol (not UK) advises never sending money or documents and telling someone where you are going to a first meeting. Do not attribute these to Report Fraud or NCSC.

## 6. Most-used dating apps

- USA - Pew Research Center, survey July 2022, 6,034 adults, published Feb 2023 (V, high confidence for 2022, dated). Ever used, share of all adults: Tinder 14%, Match 9%, Bumble 8%, OkCupid 6%, eharmony 6%, Hinge 6%, Grindr 2%, HER 1%. Among online daters: Tinder 46%, Match 31%, Bumble 28%, OkCupid/eharmony/Hinge about 20% each. No newer Pew app data found. Age 35+ breakdown not seen in results.
- UK - Ofcom, May 2024 adult reach (V via press summaries of Ofcom; medium confidence): Tinder about 1.9M, Hinge 1.4M, Bumble 1.1M, Grindr 913k, Badoo 521k. Users skew male (65/35). Over half of Plenty of Fish, Badoo and Happn users are 35-54 (useful for this audience). Grindr most popular among 54+. Secondary sources give different 2023 numbers. 2025 or 2026 Ofcom figures not found.
- Canada - no reputable ranking found. A low-quality site says Tinder, Bumble, Hinge, citing Statista without figures. Confidence low; do not publish without a better source. Statista US user survey (Tinder 37%, Bumble 35% of app users, past 12 months) seen - US only, V but paywalled.

## 7. First-date etiquette

- Who pays: all surveys are self-selected or commissioned, differing methods, so low-to-medium confidence.
  - US: YouGov poll - 35% say man should always pay, 32% say whoever initiated (V, YouGov). DatingAdvice.com survey of 950+ single Americans - 53% say splitting is the new normal (V, commercial).
  - UK: Aquacard survey of 700+ adults - 27% most common answer is split evenly (V, commercial lender); Badoo survey - 65% of young GB women like to pick up the tab (V, app-commissioned).
- Tipping US vs Canada vs UK: no source found (the search returned nothing on tipping rates).
- Meeting in public / telling someone where you are going: Europol advises telling family or friends where you are going (V, but not US/CA/UK). No US/CA/UK government source seen.
- Ghosting norms: no source found.
