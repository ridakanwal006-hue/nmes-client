## Gaps for Wolfe
- Most competitors rely on lived experience ("my divorce"). An AI character cannot claim it, so the angle must be honest craft (conversation, scam awareness).
- Nobody found combining: older and bald character, conversation skills, honest AI disclosure.
- Roman Urdu / Hinglish practical men's advice for 40+: one big Hindi-English coach for young Indian men, nothing for 40+.
- Cheap low-ticket products ($9-29) exist but are thin; mid-price ($50-300) gap.
