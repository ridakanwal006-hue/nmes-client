# Store listings (copy-paste ready)

For Gumroad, Payhip, Stan Store or any similar store. Prices are suggestions. Edit them to match what you really charge.

## Rules to follow in every listing (from the research and the FTC)
- **No outcome promises.** Never say a guide will get someone a date, a partner or any result. Say what it helps you practise.
- **No made-up reviews.** Only use real testimonials, and only with permission. Never offer a reward or a refund condition tied to a positive review.
- **Show the refund policy up front** (not buried in the terms).
- **A "bundle saves X%" claim is only honest if each guide is really sold separately at the list price.**
- **Say that Dorian Wolfe is an AI-generated character** in every listing (done below).
- **No medical, legal or financial claims.** No ED, hair-loss or testosterone claims.
- Check the store's own rules (Gumroad, Payhip, Meta ads) before you launch.
