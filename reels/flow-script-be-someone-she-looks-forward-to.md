# Naya script: "Someone she looks forward to talking to" (7 clips, Flow prompts)

Wahi idea (kam over-texting, mystery, space), lekin sachcha aur izzat wala. Koi "control" ya "messes with her mind" nahi.
Har clip ke shuru mein `reels/flow-gideon-new-account.md` ka MASTER LOCK paste karein (haath ke ishare, kaala background, seedha lens, saaf "comment" ke saath). Har clip 8 s se chhoti.
Gesture line (end mein): `He gestures naturally while talking: open palm for emphasis, hands rest on his knees between sentences.`

## Script
- **C1 (hook):** "How do you become someone she looks forward to talking to? Not by texting her all day. By being someone with a life."
- **C2:** "These three habits build real attraction. Not tricks, just balance. Number one."
- **C3:** "Say one real thing, not ten. A short, honest message beats a long, needy one."
- **C4:** "Number two. Then go live your day. Take your time before replying. People are drawn to someone who has a life, not someone who is always waiting."
- **C5:** "Number three. Come back with energy. Ask about her day, remember what she told you, and show up the same way every time."
- **C6:** "Consistency builds trust. Space builds interest. Together, they build a connection that feels natural, because it is."
- **C7 (CTA):** "Want the exact messages to send? Comment the word SPACE, and I'll send you my free playbook."

(Keyword SPACE aap badal sakti hain. "Comment" aur keyword poore lafz mein bulwayein, letters mein nahi.)

Clips banane ke baad zip bhej dein. Mein isi ending, glitch aur editing ke saath reel bana dungi, awaaz bhi waisi hi.
