# Rehearsing reel - Flow / Veo 3.1 prompts (natural speaking style)

Har clip ke liye: Look 9 ki image attach karo (Ingredients / Frames to video), 8 sec, 9:16, phir neeche ka
LOCK + us clip ki line paste karo. Chaaron clips ek hi LOCK use karen taake awaaz aur style same rahe.

## LOCK (har clip mein sabse pehle)
Same man, same room, same burgundy sweater as the reference image. Podcast interview, a friendly host sits just
off-screen to the right of the camera; he looks at the host the whole time and never into the camera lens.
Speaking style: relaxed, natural, conversational, like telling a friend a real story. Medium-low calm voice, normal
everyday speed, short natural pauses, a small breath between sentences. Mouth movement is small and subtle, lips
only slightly parted, no wide open mouth, no big teeth-showing smile while speaking, no exaggerated expressions.
Gentle small head nods, soft eyebrow movement, hands stay clasped on the table. Camera static, medium shot, soft
daylight. No text, no subtitles, no music.

## Clip 1 (hook)
He says exactly: "You rehearsed a 30 second phone call 10 times. Ten. Ten."

## Clip 2
He says exactly: "You wrote the text, deleted it, rewrote it, then asked a friend if it sounds okay."

## Clip 3
He says exactly: "That's not being careful. That's not trusting your own voice to handle a normal moment."

## Clip 4
He says exactly: "So next time, send it messy, say it imperfect. The world won't end, but the rehearsing will."

## Tip
- Pehle sirf Clip 1 banao aur dekho muh/nazar theek hai ya nahi; phir baaki teen.
- Agar muh phir bhi bohot khule to line ke saath add karo: "speaks softly, barely moving his lips".
- Chaaron clips mil jaayen to bhej do, main isi style mein dobara edit kar dunga.
