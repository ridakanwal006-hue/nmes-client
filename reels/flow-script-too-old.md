# Google Flow (Veo) script: "She said you're too old"

Paste-ready for Google Flow. Poori script, awaaz ki hidayat (voice lock) aur 5 clips ke prompts ek file mein hain.
Character Dorian Wolfe hai (AI-generated). Flow ko **character ki photo** bhi do (Ingredients / start frame), warna chehra har clip mein badal jayega.

---

## 0. Kaise use karein (Roman Urdu)
1. Flow mein naya project kholen. Dorian ki photo ko **reference / start frame** ke taur par lagayein.
2. Neeche ka **MASTER LOCK** har clip ke prompt ke shuru mein paste karein (har clip mein, kyunke Flow ko pichli clip yaad nahi rehti).
3. Phir C001 se C005 ka prompt ek ek karke generate karein. Har clip 8 second se chhoti hai.
4. Har clip mein **awaaz ek jaisi** aani chahiye. Agar kisi clip mein awaaz badal jaye (tez, patli, ya chidh-chidhi), wo clip dobara generate karein.
5. Clips ko edit mein jorein (hamara reel template: laal typewriter hook, one-word captions, glitch, drone bed).

**Ek zaroori sachai:** Flow aap ki di hui awaaz ki file se awaaz copy **nahi** kar sakta. Neeche jo awaaz ka bayan hai, wo us se milti-julti awaaz banata hai, bilkul wahi nahi. Agar bilkul wahi Brian awaaz chahiye, to Brian ka voiceover alag se banakar (vidIQ credits aane ke baad) video par lagana hoga. Us surat mein hont aur awaaz ka sync thoda mushkil hota hai, is liye is script ko "awaaz ke bina" bhi use kar sakte hain (neeche option B).

## 1. MASTER LOCK (har clip ke shuru mein paste karein)

```
MASTER LOCK. Keep identical in every clip.

CHARACTER: Dorian Wolfe. A man in his late 40s. Completely bald head, short well-groomed grey beard, calm direct eyes, strong jaw. Wears a dark charcoal suit, white shirt, no tie. Looks exactly like the reference photo. Same face, same suit, same lighting in every clip.

VOICE (this exact voice in every clip): a deep, resonant, warm male baritone. Mature, steady and comforting, like a trusted older friend talking one-to-one. Low pitch (around 100 to 110 Hz), smooth with a very slight natural gravel. Slow, measured pace with natural pauses of about half a second between sentences. Calm and confident, never shouting, never dramatic, never announcer-like. Neutral American accent. Sounds like a real person speaking to one person across a table. Intimate, close-microphone sound. No echo, no reverb, no robotic or processed tone, no pitch effects, no background music, no sound effects. Do not change the voice between clips.

SETTING: dark moody room, deep black background with a soft red glow behind him on the right. Single soft key light on his face. Subtle film grain.

CAMERA: vertical 9:16. Medium close-up, chest up, locked off tripod. Very slow push-in during the clip. He looks straight into the lens.

PERFORMANCE: calm, serious, warm. Small natural head movements, minimal hand movement. Lips synced exactly to the spoken words.

DO NOT: add on-screen text, captions, logos, subtitles, music, laugh tracks, other people, or a different voice. Do not make him smile broadly. No text on screen.
```

## 2. Poori script (referans)

```
She said you're too old. Listen carefully.
She didn't reject you.
She rejected a man who apologizes for his age.
At twenty-five, you had energy.
At forty-five, you have standards, stories, and a spine.
Stop explaining your age. Stop joking about it first.
Say it once, calmly, and move on.
The right woman isn't looking for young.
She's looking for steady.
Be steady.
```

Hook (edit mein laal typewriter): **SHE SAID YOU'RE TOO OLD.**

---

## 3. Clip prompts (har ek ke shuru mein MASTER LOCK lagayein)

### C001 (about 6 s)
```
[MASTER LOCK]

Dorian looks into the camera, serious and calm. He speaks slowly in the locked deep, resonant baritone voice:
"She said you're too old. Listen carefully."
Half-second pause. A slight steady look.
"She didn't reject you."
```

### C002 (about 7 s)
```
[MASTER LOCK]

Same shot, same lighting. Dorian continues, calm and firm, in the same deep, resonant baritone voice:
"She rejected a man who apologizes for his age."
Half-second pause. His expression softens slightly.
"At twenty-five, you had energy."
```

### C003 (about 8 s)
```
[MASTER LOCK]

Same shot. Dorian leans in very slightly, steady and warm, in the same deep, resonant baritone voice:
"At forty-five, you have standards, stories, and a spine."
Half-second pause.
"Stop explaining your age. Stop joking about it first."
```

### C004 (about 6 s)
```
[MASTER LOCK]

Same shot. Dorian speaks quietly and calmly, in the same deep, resonant baritone voice:
"Say it once, calmly, and move on."
Half-second pause. A small nod.
"The right woman isn't looking for young."
```

### C005 (about 5 s)
```
[MASTER LOCK]

Same shot. Dorian holds eye contact, voice low and steady, in the same deep, resonant baritone voice:
"She's looking for steady."
One-second pause. A faint, quiet look of confidence, not a big smile.
"Be steady."
Hold the final look for a second in silence.
```
