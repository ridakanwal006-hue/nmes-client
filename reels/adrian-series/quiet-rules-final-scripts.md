# The Quiet Rules - FINAL scripts (Muse + 1 fix), 3 clips per reel, ~18-20 s
Har clip <= 15 lafz. Flow mein har clip ke shuru mein LOW-ENERGY LOCK (neeche) paste karo, phir "He says exactly: ...".
Look: Spotlight (black sweater, dark studio). Pehle Day 1 ke 3 clips banao, phir baaki.

## LOCK (har clip ke shuru mein)
Same man, same black sweater, same dark spotlight studio as the reference image. A friendly host sits just
off-screen to the right of the camera; he looks at the host the whole time and never into the camera lens.
ENERGY: LOW and calm, reflective, quietly honest, like a late-night conversation. Not excited, no big smile, no
laughing, no raised eyebrows, no head bobbing, no hand waving. Voice low and soft, slow-to-normal pace, small
natural pauses. MOUTH: small and subtle, lips only slightly parted, teeth barely visible, no wide open mouth.
Still posture, one slow small nod at most. Static camera, medium shot, no text, no subtitles, no music.

## DAY 1 - Overthinking
A: He says exactly: "You typed the message, read it five times, changed one word, then closed the app."
B: He says exactly: "An hour later you opened it again, still staring at the same grey unsent text."
C: He says exactly: "Rule one: send the rough version. Rough and sent beats perfect and stuck."
Titles: The Unsent Draft | Rule One
Caption: Everyone has a draft folder full of almost-sent honesty. What did you almost send this week?

## DAY 2 - People-pleasing
A: He says exactly: "Your friend asked for a favour and you said yes before she even finished asking."
B: He says exactly: "You spent the whole evening resenting the plan you agreed to with a smile."
C: He says exactly: "Rule two: pause before you answer. A slow yes is still yours to give."
Titles: The Automatic Yes | Rule Two
Caption: Pleasing everyone quietly costs you yourself. When was the last time you said no and meant it?

## DAY 3 - Comparison
A: He says exactly: "He posted his promotion at noon and your whole afternoon quietly fell apart."
B: He says exactly: "You refreshed his profile twice, comparing his highlight reel to your ordinary Tuesday."
C: He says exactly: "Rule three: compare only with your past self. Yesterday is the only fair rival."
Titles: The Noon Post | Rule Three
Caption: His win didn't shrink your life, only the way you looked at it. What have you grown past this year?

## DAY 4 - Too many people for advice
A: He says exactly: "You asked your friend, your brother, and a stranger in a comment section."
B: He says exactly: "Three different answers later, you trust your own voice even less than before."
C: He says exactly: "Rule four: ask one person, then decide. Your gut was waiting to be heard."
Titles: Too Many Voices | Rule Four
Caption: Advice is cheap when everyone hands it out. Whose voice do you actually trust?

## DAY 5 - Replaying conversations at night   (Clip C badla gaya: Muse wali line confusing thi)
A: He says exactly: "It's 2 a.m. and you're still rewriting what you said at dinner tonight."
B: He says exactly: "You keep testing new versions of one sentence nobody else even remembers."
C: He says exactly: "Rule five: the conversation already ended. Put the sentence down and sleep."
Titles: The 2 A.M. Replay | Rule Five
Caption: Nobody else is awake thinking about your sentence. What would you do with that hour back?

## DAY 6 - Staying silent in a meeting
A: He says exactly: "You knew the answer in the meeting and watched someone else say it first."
B: He says exactly: "Everyone nodded at her words, and you nodded too, swallowing your own version."
C: He says exactly: "Rule six: say it while it matters. Waiting only makes the words heavier."
Titles: The Swallowed Answer | Rule Six
Caption: The room never knew what you were holding back. What will you say out loud next time?

## DAY 7 - Waiting to feel ready
A: He says exactly: "You've waited eleven months to feel ready, and ready still hasn't shown up."
B: He says exactly: "The course sits half finished, the plan sits in a note, and you sit waiting."
C: He says exactly: "Rule seven: start unready and small. Motion teaches what waiting never will."
Titles: Eleven Months Waiting | Rule Seven
Caption: Ready is a feeling, not a door that opens. What's one tiny step you could take today?
