## DAY 1 - Rule #1: Send it messy
Clip A: "You rehearsed that message ten times in your head. Then you never sent it."
Clip B: "Rule one: send it messy. A rough sentence is better than a perfect silence."
Pop titles: "NEVER SENT IT" / "SEND IT MESSY"
Caption: You rehearsed it ten times. Rule #1: send it messy. A rough sentence beats a perfect silence. Which message are you sitting on? 👇

## DAY 2 - Rule #2: Your body answers first
Clip A: "You said yes out loud while everything inside you said no. And you smiled anyway."
Clip B: "Rule two: your body answers before your manners do. Listen to it first, then decide."
Pop titles: "YOU SMILED ANYWAY" / "BODY FIRST"
Caption: Your stomach knew before your mouth did. Rule #2: listen to your body first, then decide. What did you say yes to this week? 👇

## DAY 3 - Rule #3: Don't compare your middle to their final cut
Clip A: "Everyone online looks like they have a plan. Most of them are just posting the good days."
Clip B: "Rule three: don't compare your messy middle to someone else's final cut. Yours is still being edited."
Pop titles: "THE GOOD DAYS" / "STILL BEING EDITED"
Caption: Their highlight reel vs your behind the scenes. Rule #3: your middle isn't the failure, it's the work. Save this for a bad scroll day.

## DAY 4 - Rule #4: Ask once
Clip A: "You asked six people what to do. Now you have six opinions and no answer."
Clip B: "Rule four: ask once, listen carefully, then decide with your own voice. Wrong is fixable."
Pop titles: "SIX OPINIONS" / "ASK ONCE"
Caption: More advice isn't more clarity. Rule #4: ask once, then decide. How many people have you asked this week? 👇

## DAY 5 - Rule #5: Close the tab
Clip A: "It's two a.m., and you're replaying something you said three days ago. Again."
Clip B: "Rule five: nobody remembers it the way you do. Close the tab and sleep."
Pop titles: "THREE DAYS AGO" / "CLOSE THE TAB"
Caption: Nobody replayed it but you. Rule #5: let it go and sleep. Send this to someone who overthinks at night. 🌙

## DAY 6 - Rule #6: Say it half-ready
Clip A: "You had the answer. You stayed quiet. Then someone else said it and got the credit."
Clip B: "Rule six: say it half-ready. Early and rough beats late and perfect."
Pop titles: "GOT THE CREDIT" / "HALF-READY"
Caption: The best idea in the room is useless unseen. Rule #6: speak half-ready. When did you last stay quiet? 👇

## DAY 7 - Rule #7: Start talking
Clip A: "Seven days ago, you would have rehearsed this until you gave up."
Clip B: "Rule seven: you don't need to feel ready. You just need to start talking."
Pop titles: "SEVEN DAYS" / "JUST START"
Caption: Week one done. Seven rules, one idea: you don't have to feel ready. Which rule hit you hardest? 👇 Follow for week two.
