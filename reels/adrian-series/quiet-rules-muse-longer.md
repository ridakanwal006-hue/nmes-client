# The Quiet Rules - Muse scripts, thori lambi (har clip ~14 lafz, ~7 s bolna, poori reel ~13-15 s)
Muse ke 7 scripts (adrian-cole-the-quiet-rules-series.pdf) ke topics wahi, sirf lines thori lambi.

1 Overthinking
A: "You rewrote that message ten times, then deleted it and said nothing at all."
B: "Rule one: say it badly. Badly said still beats never said. Send it."

2 People-pleasing
A: "You said yes to dinner when your whole body wanted to stay home. Again."
B: "Rule two: a tired yes helps no one. Rest is not rude. Rest is honest."

3 Comparison
A: "You scrolled past his win and suddenly your own life felt small. Just like that."
B: "Rule three: his chapter ten is not your chapter three. Keep reading yours."

4 Too much advice
A: "You asked five people what to do, and now you're more lost than before."
B: "Rule four: your gut already answered. Stop polling the room and trust the first one."

5 Replaying conversations
A: "It's midnight and you're still replaying what you said at lunch, word by word."
B: "Rule five: the conversation ended hours ago. You're the only one still in it."

6 Staying silent
A: "You had the answer in the meeting but let someone else say it first."
B: "Rule six: speak before you're sure. Certainty comes after the sentence, not before it."

7 Starting before you feel ready
A: "You've been waiting to feel ready for almost a year now. Still not ready?"
B: "Rule seven: ready is a feeling, not a requirement. Start small today. Start badly."
