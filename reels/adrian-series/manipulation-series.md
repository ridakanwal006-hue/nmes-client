# "That's manipulation" reel (1.5k) ke formula par naye scripts - 3 clips (A hook / B pattern / C naam+hal)
1.5k wali reel ka formula: (1) pehli line seedha "that's not your fault" jaisi validation, (2) 4-5 behaviours ek ke baad ek,
(3) "That's manipulation" jaisa naam, (4) aakhir mein clarity ("trust what you saw"). Topic: logon ko apni zindagi mein pehchaan.
Har clip <= 15 lafz. Look: spotlight, LOW-ENERGY murmur lock (quiet-rules-final-scripts.md).

## M1 Gaslighting
A: "If they keep saying you're remembering it wrong, that's not confusion. That's control."
B: "They deny what happened until you trust their memory more than your own."
C: "Write it down. Your memory isn't the problem. Their story keeps changing."
Cover: "REMEMBERING IT WRONG?"

## M2 Silent treatment
A: "When someone goes silent to punish you, that's not space. That's a weapon."
B: "They wait until you apologize for something you never did, just to end the quiet."
C: "Real peace doesn't require you to beg. Silence used as punishment is control."
Cover: "SILENCE AS PUNISHMENT"

## M3 Guilt trip
A: "If every boundary you set turns into you being selfish, that's manipulation."
B: "They don't argue with the boundary. They make you feel guilty for having one."
C: "Guilt isn't proof you're wrong. Sometimes it's proof they're used to your yes."
Cover: "WHY DO YOU FEEL GUILTY?"

## M4 Hot and cold
A: "If they're sweet one day and cold the next, you're not too sensitive."
B: "They keep you guessing, so you work harder for the version that was kind."
C: "Consistency is the minimum. Anything else is a pattern, not a mood."
Cover: "SWEET ONE DAY. COLD THE NEXT."

## M5 Product bridge (manipulation -> self-trust)
A: "After enough manipulation, you stop trusting your own judgment. That was the goal."
B: "They don't need you to believe them. They need you to doubt yourself."
C: "That's why I built a 14-day self-trust tracker. Link in my bio."
Cover: "THEY WANT YOU TO DOUBT YOURSELF"
Caption: Manipulation works by making you doubt yourself. Self-Doubt to Self-Trust = 13 lessons + a 14-day tracker to rebuild it, one small step a day. Link in bio. 🤍
