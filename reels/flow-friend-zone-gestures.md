## Extra gesture line (har clip ke end mein jodein)

## Clips
- **C1:** `He says: "Tell her this so she stops seeing you as just a friend. These four lines show interest without sounding desperate."`
- **C2:** `He says: "Number one. I really enjoy spending time with you. I get the feeling that knowing you deeper would be even more interesting."`

- **C4:** `He says: "Number three. I don't usually invest this much time in someone. That's why I want to get to know you better."`
- **C5:** `He says: "Number four. I enjoyed meeting you, and I won't lie. I have no intention of being just another friend in your life."`
