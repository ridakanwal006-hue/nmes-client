# Outsourced-decisions reel - Flow / Veo 3.1 prompts (natural speaking, spotlight look)

Spotlight look ki image attach karo (Ingredients / Frames to video), 8 sec, 9:16. Har clip mein LOCK + line.

## LOCK (har clip mein sabse pehle)
Same man, same black sweater, same dark spotlight studio as the reference image. A friendly host sits just
off-screen to the right of the camera; he looks at the host the whole time and never into the camera lens.
Performance: NOT theatrical, NOT an actor reciting lines. He talks like a real podcast guest thinking out loud,
tired-honest and calm. Low, quiet, conversational voice, normal everyday speed with small natural pauses and a
breath between sentences. Mouth and jaw move only a little: lips barely parted, teeth hardly visible, no wide
open mouth, no stretched or exaggerated mouth shapes, no big smile, no raised eyebrows on every word. Almost no
head movement, just a slow small nod now and then, eyes steady on the host. Static camera, medium shot, no text,
no subtitles, no music, no sound effects.

## Clip 1
He says exactly: "You asked five people what to do. Got five different answers. Now you're more confused than before."

## Clip 2
He says exactly: "That's what happens when you outsource your decisions. You collect opinions hoping one of them feels like certainty."

## Clip 3
He says exactly: "But no amount of advice replaces self-trust. You already know what you want. You're just scared to own it."

## Clip 4
He says exactly: "So decide. One voice matters here. Yours. Wrong decisions teach. No decisions just trap."

## Tips
- Pehle sirf Clip 1 banao. Agar muh ab bhi bohot khule to line ke saath add karo: "speaks softly, barely moving his lips".
- Lambi line (22+ lafz) se muh tez aur ajeeb hota hai. Agar zaroorat ho to ek clip ki line do hisson mein tod do.
- Naye clips bhej do, main isi style mein dobara edit kar dunga.
