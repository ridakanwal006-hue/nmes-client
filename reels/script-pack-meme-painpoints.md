# WOLFE script pack 2: meme-style hooks on real pain points

Same rules as `script-pack-by-product.md`: Dorian is an AI character, no personal stories, no promises, no made-up numbers, free item first. Spoken in Brian voice: contractions, "Okay, listen.", "...". Each is about 25-35 seconds (70-95 words), shorter than pack 1 so the meme hook lands.

Structure of every script: **meme hook (2-3 s) → name the pain in plain words → one real fix → keyword CTA.**
Meme formats are free to copy. Do not copy another person's clip or sound.
