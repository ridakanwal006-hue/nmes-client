# Google Flow: 4 clips (credits bachane ke liye), "She said you're too old"

**Har clip ka prompt poora alag hai** (har ek mein character, camera, awaaz, "koi host nahi", "koi dusri awaaz nahi", "music nahi", "seedha camera mein"). Bas copy karke Flow mein paste karein.

## Pehle ye karein (credits bachane ke liye)
1. Har clip mein start frame wahi: **`dorian-closeup-start-frame.png`** (chest-up, camera ki taraf dekhta hua). Wide podcast photo mat dein.
2. **Pehle sirf C001 banayein.** Dekhein: (a) nazar poori clip mein lens mein, (b) sirf ek awaaz, (c) rawaan bolna, (d) baal wahi, (e) koi zoom ya music nahi. Theek ho to C002 se C004. Theek na ho to C002 na banayein; mujhe batayein kya galat tha, main prompt badal dunga.
3. Har clip mein 1 hi baar generate karein jab tak pehla wala fail na ho.

## Script mein ek badlav (4 clips ke liye zaroori)
Flow ki ek clip 8 second ki hoti hai aur wo lagbhag 15 lafz se zyada aaram se nahi bol pata. Poori script (63 lafz) 4 clips mein nahi aati, warna lafz kat jate hain aur credits zaya hote hain. Isliye maine **2 jumle hata diye**:
- ~~Stop joking about it first.~~
- ~~Say it once, calmly, and move on.~~

Baqi sab same hai (Listen carefully, At twenty-five, Stop explaining your age sab rakhe hain). Agar aap ko wo 2 jumle bhi chahiye to ek 5th clip banani padegi: "Stop joking about it first. Say it once, calmly, and move on." (12 lafz, wahi prompt, sirf lines badlen).
