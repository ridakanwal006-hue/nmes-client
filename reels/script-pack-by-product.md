# WOLFE script pack: one reel per product, built on real search keywords

Format: spoken script (Brian voice, conversational) + on-screen hook + caption + hashtags + comment keyword.
Voice rules: contractions, "Okay, listen.", "...", short sentences. About 110-140 words = 40-50 seconds.

## 0. Rules (do not break these)
- Dorian Wolfe is an AI character. Put "AI character" in the bio and pin a comment. Never say "I was divorced", "my ex", "I dated 100 women" or any personal story.
- No promises ("she will reply", "you will get a date"). Say "practise", "try", "here's a simple way".
- Examples are labelled "for example". No made-up testimonials, no fake numbers. Only two numbers are used here and both are checked: FTC, people 60+ lost $2.4B to fraud in 2024 (all fraud types, not only romance); Pew, 64% of men say too few messages is a problem (all ages).
- Sad or hopeless topics get the line: "If it feels heavier than nerves, talk to someone. In the US, call or text 988."
- Free item first, paid item second. Reply to comments with the keyword, then ManyChat sends the link.
- Check the platform's own rules (Meta, TikTok, store) before running ads.
