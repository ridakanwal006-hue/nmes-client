# Flow prompts: baqi lines, sirf seedha camera mein dekhte hue

Wajah: pichle clips mein wo side dekhta / gardan ghumata tha, is liye wo lines reel mein nahi aa sakin.
**Start frame:** `reels/dorian-closeup-start-frame.png` (close-up). Wide podcast photo mat dein, warna mic aur host wala scene ban jata hai.
Lafz mere andaze se hain (awaaz se pakde gaye): apni asli script se milakar theek kar lein.

## MASTER LOCK (har clip ke shuru mein paste karein)
```
Dorian Wolfe, man in his late 40s, thick swept-back dark hair graying at the temples, short salt-and-pepper beard, charcoal three-piece suit, white open-collar shirt. Same face as the reference image.
Plain solid black background. No lights, no bokeh, no shelves, no microphone, no props.
Vertical 9:16, locked camera, chest-up close-up, static. He looks straight into the lens the whole time. His head stays facing front and does not turn, tilt away or look sideways. Eyes on the camera.
Only one voice: his. No host, no other person, no laughter, no "hmm", no filler sounds, no music.
Calm, serious, confident, deep warm baritone. Speaks continuously, no long pauses, ends the sentence and closes his mouth. No on-screen text or subtitles.
```

## Clips (8 s se chhoti, har ek mein 1 ya 2 jumle)
- **A:** "It's not a compliment. It's an application."
- **B:** "You're clearly a nine, but I need to know if you're actually funny, or just good at getting bored."
- **C:** "There's something about you that says you do things your own way."
- **D:** "Or try one. You've got the energy of someone who gets her friends in trouble, but nobody stays mad at you."

Har prompt = MASTER LOCK + `He says: "<line>"`. Jo clip side dekhe ya hans de, use dobara generate karein.
