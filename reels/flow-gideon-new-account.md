# Google Flow: Gideon, naye account ke liye (copy-paste)

Flow awaaz ki file copy nahi karta. Har clip ke prompt mein neeche wala MASTER LOCK paste karein, to awaaz aur chehra ek jaisa rahega.

## Pehle ye 2 cheezein lagayein
1. Naya project banayein.
2. Gideon ki saaf seedhi photo (WhatsApp wali, jo aap ne di thi) ko **start frame / Ingredient** ki tarah lagayein. Har clip mein wahi photo.

## MASTER LOCK (har clip ke shuru mein paste karein)
```
Gideon Calder, a man in his 50s with swept-back dark hair graying at the temples, a short salt-and-pepper beard, charcoal textured three-piece suit, white open-collar shirt. Same face and suit as the reference image.
Sitting in a brown leather chair, podcast microphone on the right on a boom arm. Plain solid black background. No lights, no bokeh, no shelves, no other person.
Vertical 9:16, locked camera, static, chest-up to knees framing exactly like the reference image. He looks straight into the lens the whole time. Head stays facing front, no glancing sideways. No big smile.

VOICE (identical in every clip): a mature American male voice, mid-low baritone (about 105 to 120 Hz), calm, serious, confident, with a slight natural gravel. Normal speaking pace (about 150 words per minute), continuous and natural, only a short half-second pause between sentences. Sounds like a real man speaking to one person. No echo, no reverb, no announcer tone, same voice in every clip.
Only his voice. No host voice, no second person, no laughter, no "hmm" or filler sounds, no breathing noises, no music, no sound effects.
No on-screen text, captions or subtitles. Do not spell words letter by letter.
```

## Clip likhne ka tareeqa
`[MASTER LOCK]` + `He says: "<line>"` (har clip 15 lafz ya kam, 8 s se chhoti).

## CTA ke liye khaas
Keyword ko ek lafz ki tarah bulwayein: `He says: "Comment QUESTIONS, and I'll send you my free playbook."` (QUESTIONS poora lafz, letters nahi.)

## Pehle test clip
Sirf ek chhoti clip banayein, check karein: seedha dekhe, awaaz purani jaisi, koi subtitle ya hansi nahi. Phir baqi banayein.
