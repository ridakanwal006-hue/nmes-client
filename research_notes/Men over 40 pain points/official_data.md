## Loneliness, social isolation, male friendship, help-seeking

### Takeaway
The "male friendship recession" (15% of men with no close friends) is real survey data but comes from one 2021 survey of all men 18+, not men 40+. Gallup's men-specific loneliness number is for ages 15-34, not 40+.

### Cited Findings
- Surgeon General advisory (2 May 2023): social isolation linked to 29% higher risk of premature death; loneliness 26% [secondary, news reprints; advisory itself not opened] — [Chief Healthcare Executive](https://chiefhealthcareexecutive.com/view/loneliness-leads-to-early-death-surgeon-general-warns-health-systems-can-help-); also [AP reprint](https://newschannel9.com/news/nation-world/loneliness-and-isolation-have-reached-epidemic-level-surgeon-general-says-mental-health-depression-anxiety-social-disconnection-alone-disease-illness-economic-impact-work-school-youth-elderly-marriage-church-community-social-media)
- Survey Center on American Life, American Perspectives Survey, May 2021 (reported n=2,019 adults): 15% of men have no close friends vs 3% in 1990; men with 6+ close friends fell 55% to 27%; 20% of men not in a romantic relationship have none; 10% of women have none [secondary] — [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/lifestyle/american-men-friendship-recession-report.amp), [Art of Manliness](https://www.artofmanliness.com/articles/male-friendship-recession/). Note: 2021 is during COVID; one column reports a conflicting "10+ friends" metric (40% in 1990 to 15% in 2021), a different threshold, so the two 15% figures must not be conflated.
- Gallup 2023-2024: 25% of US men aged 15-34 felt lonely "a lot of the previous day" vs 18% national average and 18% of young women; OECD median for young men 15% — [Gallup](https://news.gallup.com/poll/690788/younger-men-among-loneliest-west.aspx) (via search summary). Not a 40+ figure.
- Harvard Study of Adult Development (started 1938; 268 Harvard men + 456 Boston inner-city boys): relationship satisfaction at 50 predicted health at 80 better than midlife cholesterol (Waldinger TED claim); observational only, all-male original cohort — [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/amp/show/researchers-find-strong-relationships-protect-long-term-health-and-happiness) [secondary]

### Inferences
- The "no friends" statistic is frequently misapplied to middle-aged men; the source is an all-adult-men figure with no 40+ breakdown in what I saw.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed evidence retrieved on why men do not seek help, Pew/APA/NIH male loneliness for 40+, or UK ONS loneliness data.

## Mental health, suicide, depression, substance use

### Takeaway
Suicide rates rise for men 35+ in recent CDC provisional data; overdose deaths for males fell sharply in 2024 after a 2022-23 plateau. Depression, alcohol and ONS figures were not obtained.

### Cited Findings
- CDC/NCHS provisional 2022: age-adjusted male suicide rate 23.1 per 100,000 (22.8 in 2021); rates declined for males 34 and under and increased for those 35+ — [ABC7 summary of CDC release](https://abc7.com/suicide-cdc-number-in-america/13629806/) [secondary]. Same coverage: all-sex increases of 8.1% (65+) and 6.6% (45-64) vs 2021 [secondary].
- 2023 (final, via CDC WONDER PDF): middle-aged (as defined there) 24.6% of population but 31.4% of suicides; older adults 17.7% of population, 21.2% of suicides; all-sex — [SAVE PDF](https://save.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2023datapgsv1a.pdf) [secondary, age band definitions not seen]
- AFSP (all-sex) 2023 vs 2022: 45-54 down 1.8%, 55-64 up 0.11%, 65+ down 6.8% — [AFSP](https://afsp.org/suicide-statistics/) [secondary, not opened]
- NCHS Data Brief 522: 2023 drug overdose deaths 105,007, age-adjusted rate 31.3; male rate 44.3 per 100,000 (45.6 in 2022); rates rose for age 55+ in 2023 — [NCHS DB522](https://cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db522.htm). Data Brief 549 (Jan 2026): male rate fell 27.3% from 44.3 to 32.2 in 2024 — [NCHS DB549](https://cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db549.htm) (via search summary).

### Gaps
- Male-specific suicide rates for 45-64 and 65+, depression prevalence by sex/age (NCHS), alcohol deaths/binge drinking, ONS UK suicide data (male rate), WHO: not retrieved because fetch failed and search did not surface the figures.

## Divorce, remarriage, repartnering, online dating, scams

### Takeaway
Gray divorce about tripled since 1990 and is nearly 40% of divorcing persons; men who divorce at 50+ are about twice as likely as women to remarry within the year. Pew and FBI data exist for 50+/60+ but were not split by sex in what I saw.

### Cited Findings
- NCFMR/BGSU (Oct 2024): gray divorce rate roughly tripled since 1990; share of divorcing persons aged 50+ was 8% in 1990, now nearly 40%; 50-64 rate slightly declined in 2022 — [BGSU](https://www.bgsu.edu/news/online-media-newsroom/2024/10/bgsu-research-finds-divorce-among-older-adults-has-nearly-tripled-since-1990.html), [NCFMR FP-24-22](https://www.bgsu.edu/ncfmr/resources/data/family-profiles/FP-24-22)
- NCFMR FP-24-12 (2022 ACS): of those 50+ who divorced in last year, 2.9% also remarried in the last year; men 3.8%, women 1.9%. This is a one-year, same-year measure, not a general remarriage rate — [NCFMR FP-24-12](https://www.bgsu.edu/ncfmr/resources/data/family-profiles/FP-24-12)
- Pew (survey 2022, n=6,034, published 17 Jul 2023): 17% of US adults 50+ have ever used a dating site/app; about half of men 50-64 who tried online dating have paid, similar share for 65+ (exact percent not seen); women 50+ more negative about experiences — [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/07/17/dating-at-50-and-up-older-americans-experiences-with-online-dating/). "3% currently use" is [secondary, Global Dating Insights], unverified.
- FBI IC3 2024 (secondary coverage, report not opened): losses for people over 60 about $4.9 billion, up 43%; avg loss about $83,000; 147,000+ complaints; >7,500 lost over $100,000; romance scam losses for over-60s $389 million [secondary newsletter table, unconfirmed] — [AARP](https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/fbi-report-fraud-2024/). 2023: 6,700+ romance reports, nearly $357 million (over 60) [secondary]. Not sex-split.

### Gaps
- Widowhood stats for men, long-term remarriage rates by age/sex, Census living-alone data, FTC Sentinel data by sex/age: not retrieved.

## Physical health, work, retirement, caregiving, life expectancy

### Takeaway
US male life expectancy 75.8 vs 81.1 female (2023), gap 5.3 years. Testosterone-deficiency prevalence depends heavily on definition (0.1% to about 30%).

### Cited Findings
- NCHS Data Brief 521 (Dec 2024): 2023 life expectancy male 75.8 (74.8 in 2022), female 81.1 (80.2); gap 5.3 years; gap at 65 is 2.5 years [gap values from secondary summaries] — [NCHS DB521](https://cdc.gov/nchs/data/databriefs/db521.pdf)
- Testosterone: roughly 30% of men 40-79 (review, broad definition); MMAS (men 40-70) 6-12% with symptoms plus low T; European Male Aging Study 2.1% overall, 0.1% at 40-49 rising to 5% at 70-79 — [Can J Urol](https://mail.canjurol.com/html/free-articles/V21I3S1_09F_DrMiner.pdf), [BMC Surgery](https://www.biomedcentral.com/1471-2482/12/S1/S23). Older than 2018 (2012-2014 publications). MMAS (n=1,709): no association of ED with total or bioavailable testosterone in 625 men 55-85 — [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17698901) (2007, pre-2018). ED rates "26% men 50-59, 40% 60-69" came from an unnamed study, do not use.
- Unemployment, men 55+: 2.9% (women 55+ 3.3%), AARP digest of BLS data, July 2026; 733,000 unemployed men 55+ Feb 2026 (FRED, seasonally adjusted) — [AARP](https://www.aarp.org/pri/topics/work-finances-retirement/employers-workforce/employment-data-digest/), [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/LNS13024231). Long-term unemployed share of jobseekers 55+: 28.2% vs 26.2% for 16-54 (year ambiguous, "December"); 27.4% vs 20.3% in May 2024; not sex-split.
- Caregiving: AARP/NAC Caregiving in the US 2025: 63 million caregivers, nearly 1 in 4 adults, n=6,858; about 3 in 5 are women (implying about 40% men, inferred). 40% men figure from 2015 is older than 2018-era data — [AARP](https://www.aarp.org/caregiving/basics/caregiving-in-us-survey-2025/)

### Gaps
- Heart disease, prostate cancer, diabetes, sleep, obesity, hair loss, age discrimination (EEOC/AARP), retirement savings by sex (Fed SCF/EBRI), TRAVERSE trial, UK life expectancy: no data retrieved.

## Shaky or misquoted statistics (for the writer)
- "15% of men have no close friends": one 2021 survey, all men 18+, COVID period; confused with 10+ friends metric.
- Surgeon General "15 cigarettes a day" and 26%/29% mortality: from a meta-analysis, cited here via news only.
- Testosterone "30% of men 40+ deficient" depends on definition; strict criteria give 0.1-5%.
- FBI scam losses are for age 60+, mixed sex, and based on self-reported complaints; one search result wrongly gave $4.9B for 2023.
- Gray divorce "remarriage" 3.8% men vs 1.9% women is a same-year share, not lifetime remarriage.
