# Non-dating pains of men 40+: work, money, health, mental health, family, identity, friendship

Method note: ~13 web searches, all returned search-engine summaries; I did not open full pages (no WebFetch), so figures are as summarized from the linked pages. Several sources were secondary; flagged where so. Coverage is thin on: burnout/layoffs by gender, hair loss, joint pain, porn, gaming, custody/weekend dads, spirituality/legacy/regrets, and viral Reddit/YouTube/TikTok content. Those are listed as Gaps, not filled from memory.

## Work and money (layoffs, age bias, burnout, savings gaps, sandwich generation, divorce finances)

### Takeaway
Age bias is widely reported by workers 50+ (AARP 2026: 64%), and retirement savings among Gen X (now roughly 45-60) are low, with men's medians still small in absolute terms. Gray divorce is financially and socially costly for men. Burnout and "dread going to work" data for men 40+ were not found.

### Cited Findings
- AARP 2026 poll of 1,656 workers 50+: 64% saw or experienced age discrimination at work; 22% felt pushed out of a job; of those who saw/experienced it, 91% called it common, 36% very common. — [AARP 2026 age bias survey](https://www.aarp.org/work/age-discrimination/age-bias-survey-2026.html); reported in [WRTV](https://www.wrtv.com/?p=2625848)
- Earlier AARP survey (reported 2023): 62% reported age discrimination; 15% said they were not hired in the prior two years because of age. Different survey waves, not directly comparable to 64%. — [ABC News 4 syndicated report](https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/age-discrimination-in-the-workplace-has-increased-post-pandemic-aarp-survey-finds-struggling-to-find-jobs-older-workers-unfair-treatment-age-discrimination-in-employment-act)
- A news report on the 2026 results says 64% is unchanged since 2024 and more than 1 in 10 believe they were passed over for promotion because of age. — [WRTV](https://www.wrtv.com/?p=2625848)
- Gen X median retirement savings by gender (Alliance for Lifetime Income via Yahoo Finance): about $13,000 for men, $6,000 for women; only 14% of Gen X have access to a traditional pension. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/gen-x-behind-retirement-savings-113000243.html)
- NIRS 2023: median retirement account balance for Gen X $10,000 (definition differs from above; includes all households, so not comparable). — [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/personal-finance/25/01/43312785/gen-x-is-labeled-the-economys-struggling-middle-child-carrying-significant-financial-burdens-median-retirement-balance-is-only-10000)
- Schroders 2024: Gen Xers expect to accumulate about $602,944 vs a stated need of roughly $1.07 million (largest gap of any generation per the source). — [401k Specialist](https://401kspecialistmag.com/gen-xers-nearing-retirement-report-median-savings-of-50k/)
- Athene survey of "sandwich" adults 40-59 supporting both adult children and older relatives: 73% had adjusted retirement goals (commercial insurer survey). — [Athene PDF](https://www.athene.com/binaries/content/assets/ausa-assets/athene-in-the-news/bp512202564241athene.pdf)
- Gray divorce (50+), Health and Retirement Study: women's standard of living fell more, but men's losses lasted longer; both lost about half their wealth; repartnering restored women's but not men's wealth (paraphrased from search summary). — [Rutgers/Crowley summary](https://bloustein.rutgers.edu/two-different-divorces-dr-jocelyn-elise-crowley-analyzes-how-divorce-over-the-age-of-50-produces-different-impacts-for-men-and-women-in-thrive-magazine-winter-spring-2026/); [J Gerontol B](https://academic.oup.com/psychsocgerontology/article/76/10/2073/5903434)

### Inferences
- "Fear of ending up poor" is supported by data on thin savings and shortfalls, but most figures are household or Gen X-wide, not men-only.
- Age-bias figures cover workers 50+; men 40-49 are not isolated.
- Creators can responsibly say: age bias is commonly reported, start/upgrade savings now, see a fee-only financial planner. Specific investment, tax or legal/divorce advice is for professionals.

### Gaps
- No data found on layoffs by age/gender, burnout, "dread going to work", or reinvention success rates.
- Pew sandwich-generation figures were not found (search did not return Pew).
- No custody/weekend-dad, child-support or settlement data found.

## Health (cardio, metabolic, sleep, hearing, hormones, doctor avoidance)

### Takeaway
Obesity peaks in the 40-59 band; many men skip checkups; undiagnosed sleep apnea is likely common in midlife men; the "low T" industry is criticized by experts and regulators. Diabetes, prostate, hair-loss, joint data were not obtained.

### Cited Findings
- Obesity prevalence highest at ages 40-59 (46.4%), same pattern in men and women (CDC-linked report, Aug 2021-Aug 2023). — [ABC30 report on CDC data](https://abc30.com/post/obesity-prevalence-among-us-adults-falls-slightly-40-remains-higher-10-years-ago-cdc/15348080/)
- Hypertension (2017-2018 crude): 52.5% of men vs 44.0% of women (no 40s/50s breakdown in snippet). — [CDC Stacks](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/104708/cdc_104708_DS1.pdf)
- Cleveland Clinic surveys: 44% of men do not get an annual physical (2023, via WebMD podcast summary); 2022 survey: 38% of men concerned about cancer, 32% about sexual health; of men with sexual health concerns 71% also had cardiovascular disease or diabetes; a quarter of average-risk men 50+ not screened for prostate or unsure. Figures vary across secondary sources. — [Cleveland Clinic 2022](https://newsroom.clevelandclinic.org/2022/09/07/cleveland-clinic-survey-reveals-mens-top-health-concerns-as-they-age); [2024 survey](https://newsroom.clevelandclinic.org/2024/09/04/national-cleveland-clinic-survey-examines-generational-divide-in-mens-health?os=TMB); [WebMD](https://www.webmd.com/podcasts/20240111/top-tips-for-men-to-take-control-of-their-health-from-screenings-to-mental-health)
- Sleep apnea, Australian male-health cohort: diagnosed prevalence rose from 2.2% (18-25) to 7.8% (45-55); the same paper cites estimates that undiagnosed OSA in men 40-69 could be as high as 49%. — [PMC5103243](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5103243/)
- Chinese study of 794 men aged 40-65: hearing impairment in about 45-47% of intermediate/high OSA-risk men vs 33.3% low-risk; authors note the OSA-hearing link is debated; single-country sample. — [PMC9122213](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9122213/)
- Low-T marketing: FDA required heart attack/stroke warnings on testosterone labels; testosterone replacement is approved for hypogonadism due to defined causes; telehealth "male menopause" sites cite a 2002 study (levels falling about 1%/yr after 40) that a researcher says included ill older men; an endocrinologist quoted by NPR called low-T clinics "sex hormone factories"; the 2015 "TV ad ban" claim in one source is from an older, weaker summary - verify. — [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/news/article/telehealth-male-menopause-testosterone-replacement-risks/); [Healthcare Finance News](https://www.healthcarefinancenews.com/news/adverse-potential-brings-warning-booming-low-t-products)
- Testosterone supplement market estimated $1.85B in 2023 (market-research figure, unverified). — via same search; no FDA supplement-scam case found.

### Inferences
- Men often ignore symptoms; sleep apnea, BP, cholesterol, A1c and PSA decisions are all clinician territory.
- Creators can say: get a checkup/labs, ask about sleep apnea if snoring and tired, be skeptical of "low T" ads and supplements. They must not diagnose, recommend testosterone, supplements or doses, or interpret labs.

### Gaps
- No reliable 40+ male figures for diabetes, cholesterol, prostate cancer, hair loss, joint pain/injury, hearing loss prevalence, fear-of-doctor survey beyond Cleveland Clinic.

## Mental health (depression, suicide, therapy avoidance, alcohol, gambling, porn, screens)

### Takeaway
Men's depression is often untreated, midlife men are over-represented in suicide and substance deaths, but several specific numbers could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- NCHS data brief (2015, 2010-2013 data): close to 1 in 10 US men has depression or anxiety, fewer than half get treatment. NCHS 2017: 59% of men with daily anxiety/depression neither take medication nor talk to a professional. — [Health Policy Ohio on CDC](https://www.healthpolicyohio.org/health-policy-news/2015/06/12/most-men-with-depression-do-not-get-treated-cdc-finds); [CDC NCHS blog](https://blogs.cdc.gov/nchs/2017/06/08/3686/)
- 2021-23 NCHS (secondary summary): about 10% of men and 16% of women had depression; about 40% of those with depression got counseling or therapy (overall, not men-only). — [MD Psych Foundation summary](https://mdpsychfoundation.org/cdc-report-says-us-depression-rates-are-increasing-but-most-people-with-depression-are-not-receiving-therapy/)
- Older MMWR: depression 7% in men 40-59 vs 5% in men 60+. — [CDC MMWR](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm6051a7.htm)
- CDC 2023: male suicide rate 22.7 per 100,000 (23.0 in 2022); highest among men 75+. A save.org summary of CDC WONDER: middle-aged people were 24.6% of 2023 population but 31.4% of suicides (both sexes). — [CDC NCHS blog](https://cdc.gov/nchs/blog/posts/2025/09/suicide-rates-largely-unchanged-in-2023-but-still-high.html); [save.org](https://save.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2023datapgsv1a.pdf). A 29.5/100k figure for men 45-64 appeared in a therapy blog and is unverified.
- US midlife death rates from drugs, alcohol and suicide worsened 1990-2019 (Int J Epidemiology 2024). — via [search summary](https://www.healthpopuli.com/2015/11/03/death-rates-rise-for-middle-age-white-men-economics-and-mental-health-in-america/) (weak link; verify)
- Alcohol: CDC report (2015) said about three-quarters of alcohol poisoning deaths were adults 35-64, most men; UK Drinkaware says ONS data show midlife men at highest risk of alcohol-related death. — [CBS SF](https://cbsnews.com/amp/sanfrancisco/news/middle-aged-adults-not-college-kids-make-up-majority-of-drinking-deaths); [Drinkaware](https://www.drinkaware.co.uk/little-less/)
- Sports betting: a 2026 unreviewed conference abstract found suicide rates among men 35-54 fell about 2.4 per 100,000 per year after legalization (rose among 15-34); preliminary. — [UNLV](https://oasis.library.unlv.edu/gaming_institute/2026/May27/14)

### Inferences
- The "men don't seek help" claim is supported by treatment-gap data, but reasons (shame, stoicism) came from no source I opened; treat as common opinion.
- Creators can say: normalize talking, point to 988 (US) / local crisis lines, GP/therapist. Must leave diagnosis, medication, addiction treatment and suicide-risk assessment to professionals; follow safe-messaging guidance.

### Gaps
- No data found on porn, gaming/screen escape, anger, or gambling prevalence in men 40+. Mortality figures above are not cleanly 45-64 male.

## Family (estrangement, custody, parents, marriage)

### Takeaway
Estrangement is common and fathers appear over-represented; divorced men are lonelier than widowed men. Custody and parental-grief data are missing.

### Cited Findings
- Pillemer 2020 survey (1,300+ adults): 27% estranged from a close relative; up to 10% cut off from a parent or child. — [Economist summary](https://econ.st/3ZwsNOJ)
- A PMC study: 6% reported estrangement from mothers vs 26% from fathers; 69% of estranged children later reconciled with fathers in subsequent waves (retrospective, cross-sectional). — [PMC10254574](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10254574)
- YouGov 2025 (about 4,400): 38% had experienced estrangement; different question, not comparable. — via [Together Estranged](https://togetherestranged.org/why-estrangement-occurs) search results
- Gray divorce: divorced men lonelier than widowed men; repartnering narrows gap; Crowley's interviews: men more socially isolated, women more worried about finances. — [J Gerontol B 2021](https://academic.oup.com/psychsocgerontology/article/76/10/2073/5903434); [Rutgers](https://bloustein.rutgers.edu/two-different-divorces-dr-jocelyn-elise-crowley-analyzes-how-divorce-over-the-age-of-50-produces-different-impacts-for-men-and-women-in-thrive-magazine-winter-spring-2026/)

### Inferences
- Note the 26% figure is estrangement ever, including temporary; do not present as current.
- Creators can share reconciliation themes; legal custody and family-therapy needs go to lawyers/therapists.

### Gaps
- Nothing on weekend dads, custody outcomes, caring for ageing parents, grief, marriage drift.

## Identity, meaning and the midlife dip

### Takeaway
Economics research finds a U-shaped life-satisfaction curve with a low point in midlife (disputed by some psychologists).

### Cited Findings
- Blanchflower and Graham: statistically significant U-shapes across countries (Gallup World Poll 2005-2019 among sources); dip comparable in size to job loss or widowhood. — [NBER/GLO](https://glabor.org/the-mid-life-dip-in-well-being-a-critique-a-new-glo-discussion-paper-by-glo-fellows-david-g-blanchflower-carol-graham/)
- US men's happiness minimum reported in early 50s; Europe both sexes mid-40s (about 47 in media coverage). — [Fox News summary](https://www.foxnews.com/lifestyle/mid-life-crisis-may-be-culmination-of-lifes-peak-of-misery-study-reveals.amp); [CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/health/middle-age-misery-peaks-at-47-years-of-age-study-says-1.4768198)
- Dispute: other psychology papers say the U-shape is overblown (Galambos et al. 2020), contested by Blanchflower and Graham. — [PMC8525618](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8525618)

### Inferences
- "Midlife dip" is average, not destiny; creators can say it is common and often lifts.

### Gaps
- No sourced content on "invisible man", spirituality, legacy, or bucket-list regrets; no viral post coverage.

## Friendship

### Takeaway
Survey Center on American Life (2021) found a sharp decline in men's close friendships; Pew 2025 disagrees that men are lonelier than women.

### Cited Findings
- Men with 6+ close friends fell from 55% (1990) to 27%; men with none rose from 3% to 15%; 1 in 5 unmarried, unpartnered men has no close friends (May 2021 survey, all ages). — [Fox News on the report](https://www.foxnews.com/lifestyle/american-men-friendship-recession-report.amp)
- Pew (2024 survey, pub. Jan 2025): men do not report feeling lonely more often or having fewer close friends than women; adults under 50 more often lonely than 50+ (22% vs 9%). — [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/social-trends/2025/01/16/men-women-and-social-connection)
- AARP notes need for friendship among retired men and difficulty making connections after 50. — [AARP](https://www.aarp.org/home-family/friends-family/info-2023/male-friendships)

### Inferences
- Evidence is mixed: "male friendship recession" is contested; avoid stating it as settled.

### Gaps
- No evidence reviewed on what interventions help (men's sheds, clubs, etc.).
