# South Asian men 40+ (Pakistan, India, diaspora): pain points and content opportunity

Method note: about 13 tool calls (WebSearch snippets, vidIQ). Search is English-only/US-based, so Urdu/Hindi-language press and commentary were barely reached. Most findings are search summaries, not full-page reads. Nothing here is specific to age 40+ unless stated; age-specific data was mostly not found.

## Marriage, divorce, second marriage, custody, rishta

### Takeaway
Divorce/khula filings are rising in Pakistan's courts (especially Sindh and Punjab), but there is no national database and the rise partly reflects more filing. Men's pain shows up as family-court exposure, legal-misuse grievances (India: 498A/dowry), custody/access fears, and second-marriage friction. Direct evidence on men 40+ and rishta is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Punjab khula cases: 13,299 (2012), 16,942 (2014), 18,901 (2016); no later Punjab figures found. Sindh: 11,639 khula cases instituted in 2020, rising to 16,287 the next year; Dawn reported 5,189 women applied for khula in Karachi family courts in 2020; Rawalpindi district courts logged 10,312 divorce/khula/guardianship/maintenance cases Jan-Nov 2021. Pakistan has no single national divorce database; rises may reflect awareness and easier legal access — [Divorce in Pakistan (Wikipedia, secondary)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divorce_in_Pakistan); [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2621566/when-walking-away-is-the-only-option); [Daily Times](https://dailytimes.com.pk/849318/survey-says-divorce-rate-hits-all-time-high-in-pakistan/). Figures came via a search summary, so verify before quoting. A "35% rise 2014-2019" claim came from a low-quality site; excluded.
- Second marriage: a Karachi matrimonial service said failed marriages hurt men too but remarrying is easier for men than women; some divorced men want a "better" second wife partly to show the ex they moved on; 60-year-old divorced men have asked for young unmarried brides (older article) — [Express Tribune "Shaadi 2.0"](https://tribune.com.pk/story/379627/shaadi-2-0?amp=1)
- A husband's second marriage is often seen as a failure of the first marriage; women oppose partly over financial security — [Pak Observer](https://pakobserver.net/pakistani-societys-acceptance-of-remarriage/) (commentary)
- Legal risk: in Pakistan a man needs first wife's permission to remarry; marrying without it can bring jail and fines — [Daily Pakistan](https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/25-Sep-2025/95-of-men-desire-second-message-says-pakistani-actress-saima-qureshi); [Aaj News case](https://english.aaj.tv/news/30302492)
- UK diaspora: of 52 Pakistani divorcees in a British study, 30 had remarried; only six of the 22 who stayed single were men (small sample) — [ISA Global Dialogue](https://globaldialogue.isa-sociology.org/articles/divorce-in-the-diaspora)
- India: men's-rights groups and a Supreme Court PIL (after the Atul Subhash suicide, Bengaluru) claim dowry/498A laws are misused, and that fathers are denied custody/visitation while paying heavy maintenance. The IPC provision is now Section 85 BNS (from 1 July 2024). No independently verified false-case or custody-outcome data found; women's groups dispute the framing — [The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/governance/men-face-false-dowry-domestic-violence-cases-pil-in-supreme-court); [MyJoyOnline](https://www.myjoyonline.com/a-mans-suicide-leads-to-clamour-around-indias-dowry-law/)

### Inferences
- "Dusri shaadi" is both a real pain point (loneliness, widowers, divorced men) and a charged topic (first-wife consent, legal risk, women's backlash). Framing around a "second chance", honesty and obligations to existing family is safer than framing around entitlement.
- India's men's-rights grievance is real as a grassroots movement but contested; a creator would risk being read as anti-women if they adopt it uncritically.

### Gaps
- No data on joint-family and in-law conflict specific to men 40+, widower remarriage, or what men 40+ say about rishta (aunties/rishta agencies, age/income demands). Nothing on custody/access in Pakistan. No Gulf-diaspora divorce data. Nothing from Dawn/BBC Urdu/Scroll was reached.

## Loneliness, mental-health stigma, suicide, substances

### Takeaway
Indian NCRB data show men are about 73% of suicide deaths and most male victims are married, with "family problems" the leading recorded cause. Pakistan has no comparable usable male-specific data in what I found; stigma evidence is small and old.

### Cited Findings
- India 2023: over 1.71 lakh suicides, men nearly 73% — [Legal Service India](https://www.legalserviceindia.com/Legal-Articles/male-suicide-crisis-in-india-nobody-wants-to-discuss/) (legal article citing NCRB; moderate reliability)
- 2019: 68.4% of male suicide victims were married vs 62.5% of female — [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2020/09/01/suicide-cases-in-india-ncrb-report.html) (as summarised by search). 2015: of 86,808 married suicide victims, 64,534 (74%) were men — [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2024/12/25/the-reasons-for-male-suicides-in-india-what-the-numbers-tell-us)
- 2015-2022 analysis: "family problems" were 23.06% of all suicides (NCRB does not define the category); marriage-related issues 3.28% of male vs 9.66% of female suicides — [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2024/12/25/the-reasons-for-male-suicides-in-india-what-the-numbers-tell-us)
- A Supreme Court petition claimed 2021: 33.2% of male suicides from family problems, 4.8% marriage-related — petitioner's claim, unverified — [The Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/governance/men-face-false-dowry-domestic-violence-cases-pil-in-supreme-court)
- Pakistan: a 38-patient Lahore study found both sexes report high depression stigma, women more internalised — [PMC4744295](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4744295). A 2011 review put mean depression prevalence at 21.7% in men vs 45.5% in women, and a paper noted women seek help more and diagnostic criteria may skew to female symptoms — [JCPSP](https://jcpsp.pk/archive/2011/Dec2011/10.pdf); [Tehqeeqat](https://tehqeeqat.org/downloadpdf/17512). Both old and small.

### Inferences
- The married-man suicide skew plus "family problems" supports a "silent household pressure" angle, but cause categories are police-recorded and crude.
- Under-reported male depression in Pakistan is plausible from the diagnostic-bias point, but I found no direct "mard ko dard nahi hota" source.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan male suicide statistics, no WHO Pakistan/India male figures, no substance-use data, no loneliness survey for South Asian men 40+, no diaspora (UK/US/Gulf) mental-health data. "Mard ko dard nahi hota" as a phrase was not sourced.

## Money and duty, Gulf migration

### Takeaway
Economic stress is documented anecdotally and in a medical letter; Gulf separation research is small, old, and wife/children-focused. Evidence specifically on men 40+ as breadwinners is mostly anecdotal.

### Cited Findings
- Pakistan inflation peaked at 35.4% in 2023 and was 12.6% in June 2024; a Karachi medical letter links inflation/poverty/joblessness to reported suicides (mostly young people) — [PMC11420284](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11420284/); [Daily Pakistan](https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/08-Aug-2024/can-pakistan-s-inflation-crisis-be-linked-to-the-recent-increase-in-suicides). One Islamabad daily-wager quote ("I think of my children and wife") — anecdote.
- 2022 study of 330 Indian Gulf return migrants: distress linked to returning as a "failure", feeling valueless, no suitable job at home — [IJCMPH](https://www.ijcmph.com/index.php/ijcmph/article/view/10358) (per search summary; verify)
- Left-behind-family harms (insecurity, child behaviour, substance use) in Pakistan literature; a 1991 Karachi study — [JPMA](https://archive.jpma.org.pk/article-details/5214); few mental health studies of Gulf migrant workers (2012 review) — [QScience](https://qscience.com/content/papers/10.5339/qfarf.2012.AHPS2)

### Inferences
- Returning migrants in their 40s-50s (job loss, status loss, savings gone) look like an under-served pain point, but the evidence is thin.

### Gaps
- No data on supporting parents/siblings, job loss at 40+, remittance obligations, or UK/US South Asian men's financial duty. Gulf separation findings for the men themselves (loneliness in labour camps) not found.

## Health: heart, diabetes, ED, hakeems

### Takeaway
South Asian men face markedly higher cardiometabolic risk at younger ages and lower BMI; sexual-health marketing by hakeems is widespread and regulated but enforcement evidence is weak.

### Cited Findings
- UK South Asian men develop CVD about 8 years earlier than white men (60.4 vs 68.2); up to 40-50% of South Asians may have diabetes by 80; type 2 diabetes risk equals white risk at BMI ~23.9 vs 30; up to 40% undiagnosed; CHD death in diabetics ~3x higher than white diabetics — [Buckinghamshire DPH Report 2022](https://www.buckinghamshire.gov.uk/health-wellbeing-and-sports/health-and-wellbeing/joint-strategic-needs-assessment/director-of-public-health-annual-reports/director-of-public-health-annual-report-2022-preventing-heart-disease-and-stroke-in-buckinghamshire/32-differences-between-ethnic-groups)
- Older UK survey: diabetes in Pakistani men 22.4% and Bangladeshi 26.6% vs Indian 15.2%; MI prevalence in Pakistani men 55+ was 19% — [Diabetes on the Net](https://diabetesonthenet.com/wp-content/uploads/dpc15-1-20-8.pdf) (older data, 2000s-2010s)
- PEMRA (2016, Lahore complaints council) told cable operators to stop indecent hakeem/black-magic adverts — [Journalism Pakistan](https://www.journalismpakistan.com:443/Cable-operators-told-to-stop-adverts-of-hakeems-black-magic). DRAP asked PTA to block pages selling sexual-health/weight/mental-health medicines (undated) — [Pakistan Observer](https://pakobserver.net/?p=750097) (as returned by search, tie uncertain). 1941 Punjab act classifies "nervous debility"/sexual-complaint ads as indecent; current status unverified — [IndiaCode](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/21899/1/mc_-_punjab_suppression_of_indecent_advertisement_1.pdf)
- Hakeem content on YouTube Shorts is actively targeting second marriage + "mardana taqat" (see online section).

### Inferences
- "Heart/sugar check at 40" is a defensible, non-taboo health hook for desi men. ED is a large market but already saturated by quack ads; a creator would compete on trust.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan/India prevalence (NFHS, PDHS, IDF), nothing on hair loss or ED prevalence/taboo surveys, no US/Gulf data.

## Online behaviour, platforms, content, creators

### Takeaway
Pakistan's social audience is heavily male on Facebook; Hindi/Urdu "second marriage / shohar / biwi" Shorts get big views but mostly from religious-advice and drama channels, not men's self-improvement creators. Evidence of a clear men-40+ audience is indirect.

### Cited Findings
- Pakistan: 79.9M social media identities (Oct 2025, 31.2% of population) — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Pakistan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan). Facebook ~66.0M users (Sept 2026 estimate), 75.5% men; Instagram ~26.6M (Aug 2026) — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/facebook-users-in-pakistan/) (ad-audience estimates, volatile, don't mix with DataReportal). No YouTube/TikTok/WhatsApp numbers or age splits found.
- vidIQ YouTube search for Roman-Urdu "dusri shaadi" queries (sorted by views, 2024+), observed on one query:
  - "Dusri Shaadi || Second Marriage" Shorts by Lubena Firdos (447K subs): 1.66M views — [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PN-250kGD1M)
  - Mufti Tariq Masood clip "Nafarman biwi ko sudharne ka nushka" on Taqwa Network (3,240 subs): 952K views, outlier score ~30,732 — [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8O6j2SgvjaI)
  - Hindi-script "shauhar ka kisi gair aurat se talluq" (Maulana Saadi Qasmi, 628K subs): 430K — [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZMSy11yVTDc)
  - "Hakeem Babar ke baad dusri shaadi" Short with "mardana taqat" tags: 15K views — [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pQoTORk7_UM) (channel 92.6K subs; low views, spammy tag description)
  - Many dream-interpretation ("khwab mein shohar ki dusri shadi") and wazifa videos also appear — mostly women-targeted content.
- vidIQ Hindi outliers (keyword "purush shaadi talaak", last 6 months, long-form): top results were AI/scripted drama channels about wives demanding divorce (e.g. 1.67M views, 23K-sub channel "Maharani Ka Guroor") and Aniruddhacharya divorce discourse (16.7K) — [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8j78nYES6q0); [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=81z2Hu1xirA). A second query on men's mental health returned mostly celebrity news and drama; no real men's-advice outlier found.
- Marriage/divorce-themed Hindi drama stories, religious preachers and hakeems dominate attention in this niche (observed, small sample of two vidIQ queries each).

### Inferences
- High-view "second marriage / husband" content skews to Islamic Q&A and drama; this suggests demand for the topic but also that it is currently framed through religion and entertainment, leaving a gap for practical, non-judgmental Roman Urdu/Hinglish men's advice. Audience gender of these videos is unknown (they may be watched mostly by wives).
- Facebook (75% male in Pakistan) and YouTube Shorts look like the most plausible reach platforms for men 40+; this is inference, not an age-verified finding.

### Gaps
- Did not identify big men's self-improvement/relationship creators in Urdu/Hindi/Hinglish (no named list), no Instagram/TikTok reel outlier search run, no WhatsApp group evidence, no age-segmented platform data, no UK/US/Gulf usage data.

## Cultural cautions and ethics

### Takeaway
Main risks are legal (second marriage without consent; indecent sexual-health advertising rules), reputational (anti-women framing), and health-claim risk. Evidence here is mostly from the findings above; no dedicated source on pre-marital dating or sex-advice law was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Second marriage without first wife's permission can bring jail/fines in Pakistan — [Daily Pakistan](https://en.dailypakistan.com.pk/25-Sep-2025/95-of-men-desire-second-message-says-pakistani-actress-saima-qureshi)
- Sexual-debility ads historically restricted; PEMRA/DRAP actions above — sources as in Health section.
- India's men's-rights claims on 498A are contested and unverified at national level — [MyJoyOnline](https://www.myjoyonline.com/a-mans-suicide-leads-to-clamour-around-indias-dowry-law/)

### Inferences
- Avoid promoting or normalising second marriage as a "solution", dating advice, or herbal/sexual remedies; avoid suicide-method detail and follow safe-messaging (helplines). Do not present unverified suicide-cause claims from advocacy as fact. Content that encourages marriages hidden from a wife could expose the creator to harm complaints.
- Pakistan PECA and India IT Rules/obscenity law could apply to sex-related content; not verified here.

### Gaps
- No source on PECA/obscenity enforcement against advice creators, platform policy for sexual-health content, or dating-before-marriage norms for diaspora men. Needs a targeted legal check.
